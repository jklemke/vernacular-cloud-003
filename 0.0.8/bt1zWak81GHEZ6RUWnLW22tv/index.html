Definition

The saviour is the Name of God as the one who rescues: from sin, from death, from a fallen world.

Is there a saviour? No, but there is emancipation, release from an inherited bond, and transcendence, the move to a generalized perspective. They live in EPIPHANY, with edification-piety-emancipation, and they come without a haven: the spell that promises one is the Myth of the Haven.

History Note

  • "I, I am the LORD, and besides me there is no saviour" (Isaiah 43:11, NRSVue).
  • The Buddhist path to liberation, walked without a saviour: the Buddhas only point the way (Dhammapada 276).
  • The gnostic saviour descends to wake the soul trapped in a fallen world (the Apocryphon of John). Wikipedia: Gnosticism
  • Soteriology, the doctrine of salvation. Wikipedia: Soteriology

Scope Note

The saviour stands on one side of the choice that EPIPHANY poses: escape from a fallen world, or a full human life within it. Emancipation can be had without a haven.
