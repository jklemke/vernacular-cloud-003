The Badinagean Nomenclature

This is an effort to evangelize the philosophical nomenclature of Victor Badinage.

Each concept in the Badinagean nomenclature is published in SKOS, JSON-LD, HTML format. Its permanent IRI has the form https://vernacular.cloud/{UUID}/ and leads to the latest version; each version is kept at https://vernacular.cloud/{version}/{UUID}/.
