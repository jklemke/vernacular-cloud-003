Definition

Education is the jurisdiction that institutionalizes the transfer of knowledge: to the children, to the misinformed, to the purveyors of "bad" ideology. Its hard question is how to tell the dissemination of knowledge from propaganda, sophistry, and bullshit.

It is the institution, distinct from edification (Bildung), the formation of a person, which belongs to SOCIETY. Its tribunals are the examination and accreditation; its present crisis is the collapse of the Humboldtian university.

History Note

  • Plato sets Socrates against the sophists, who teach for a fee (Protagoras; Gorgias).
  • Humboldt's University of Berlin unites research and teaching (1810).
  • John Henry Newman: a university teaches universal knowledge (The Idea of a University, 1852).
  • John Dewey: education as the continual reconstruction of experience (Democracy and Education, 1916).
  • Harry Frankfurt: the bullshitter is indifferent to the truth (On Bullshit, 1986). Wikipedia: On Bullshit

Scope Note

Education is not edification, and not ideology, though it is always at risk of becoming indoctrination. Technology training and liberal education are both education; the question is what each transfers, and to whom.
