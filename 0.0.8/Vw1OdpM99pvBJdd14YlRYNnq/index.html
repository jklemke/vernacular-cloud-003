Definition

A physical realm is an interpretive framework that explores a foundational pattern in the mind-independent world. The seven Physical Realms are kinetics, molecularity, cohesion, cosmology, metabolism, physiology, and ecology. Pattern is a priori; these frameworks interpret its best-established a posteriori examples, the center of the web of belief.

Frameworks and disciplines are many-to-many. Molecularity interprets quarks, chemical elements, and DNA; every other realm presupposes kinetics. The seven form a ladder of supervenient emergence: each realm obeys the patterns of the realms beneath it and introduces new patterns of its own.

History Note

  • W. V. O. Quine pictures science as a fabric whose interior is least exposed to revision ("Two Dogmas of Empiricism", 1951, §6); with J. S. Ullian, The Web of Belief (1970). Wikipedia: Two Dogmas of Empiricism
  • Carl Sagan's Cosmos (1980) brings the established picture, from atoms to galaxies to evolution, to a television audience of hundreds of millions. Wikipedia: Cosmos: A Personal Voyage
  • The Santa Fe Institute (founded 1984) makes complexity and emergence a science across disciplines. Wikipedia: Santa Fe Institute
  • George F. R. Ellis argues for top-down causation: higher levels constrain and enable the lower (How Can Physics Underlie the Mind?, 2016). Wikipedia: George F. R. Ellis
  • Sean Carroll, following Frank Wilczek, calls the Standard Model plus general relativity the "Core Theory", and argues that the laws underlying everyday life are completely known (The Big Picture, 2016). Wikipedia: The Big Picture (Carroll book)

Scope Note

The realms are not disciplines, and the list is not an argument for materialism. Supervenient emergence means strictly following the patterns of the lower level while introducing new patterns, and new causes, at the higher: a star's macrostate drives the fusion of its hydrogen, and no planet's orbit can be calculated without a centre of gravity. Carroll is cited for the corpus, not for his view of emergence as merely epistemic. Nothing is normative until metabolism, where survival begins and the ladder continues into the Practical Realms.
