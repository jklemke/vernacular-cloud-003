concept: oubliation

concept-key: https://vernacular.cloud/0.0.1/Cu6nGt9CyXGdNG4izDYqFlof/

Definition

The Lord's Leap as the classical oubliational move in the Socratic-Abrahamic tradition.

!Victor Badinage Quotes by Theme#^cf620dThis is accomplished by making it a matter of orthodoxy, of identity politics. The Occidental theorists open the way for the opportunistic nihilists. An alliance between priest, merchant, and warrior to manipulate the Lord.

To eliminate an essential concept from one's foundation vocabulary. Classical example is the Churchland's oubliation (elimination) of consciousness. The opposite of discernment. The void

that is exposed in the act of focusing on the isolated and the granular.

To actively ignore. The third person scientist must oubliate their own subjectivity.

The Lord's Leap as the oubliation of justice.

The Socratic Miscarriage as the oubliation of subjectivity.

To select is to omit, to suspend is to oubliate. The apeiron is that which has been oubliated. See Aristotle on contraries and contradictories. Abstraction from involves selecting similarity and omitting difference. The MotP involves using logic to explain what has been oubliated. Compare MotG, MotH.

We have an esoteric battle between eliminationists and oubliationists. Rorty allows his devotion to language to oubliate ipseity. Dennett allows his devotion to science to oubliate it. The battle between right-wing and left-wing Rorteans.
