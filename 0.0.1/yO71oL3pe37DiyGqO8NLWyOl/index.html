concept: myth

Definition

A narrative, conceptual, transcendent explanation that--while not necessarily true in the sense of corresponding to actuality--bears a certain pragmatic, edificational message. The varieties of metaphysics.

Perhaps all the Myths can be seen as attempts to retrieve the oubliated apeiron and oubliated ipseity

Can we participate in logic without falling under the spell of the Myth of the Proven?

Can we participate in sentience without falling under the spell of the Myth of the Given?

Can we participate in society without falling under the spell of the Myth of the Coven?

Can we participate in legitimacy without falling under the spell of the Myth of the Maven?

Can we participate in [ostension]] without falling under the spell of the Myth of the Nomen?

Can we participate in culture without falling under the spell of the Myth of the Scriven?

Can we participate in edification without falling under the spell of the Myth of the Haven?

We are offering a myth. The strange loop of the Badinagean myth. (Remember Sophie's World)
