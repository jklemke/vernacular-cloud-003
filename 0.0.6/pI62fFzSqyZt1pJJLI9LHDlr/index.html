Definition

Art is a diverse range of [cultural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture "Culture") activity centered around [works](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Works_of_art "Works of art") utilizing [creative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creativity "Creativity") or [imaginative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imagination "Imagination") talents, which are expected to evoke a worthwhile experience,1(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art#cite_note-1) generally through an expression of emotional power, conceptual ideas, technical proficiency, or [beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beauty "Beauty").
