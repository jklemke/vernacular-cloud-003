Definition

We are using reality as a normative category that includes ENTIRETY, actuality, existence, world, universe. "Reality" is normative in the sense that one must presuppose a notion of reality in order to operate in the sentient and sapient realms.

Realism is a shifty term, for instance mathematical realism. Naive realism. What is introduced is a norm that grounds the practice of corroboration.
