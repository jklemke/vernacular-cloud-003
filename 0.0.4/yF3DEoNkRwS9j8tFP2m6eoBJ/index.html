Definition

discernment as a synonym for Kantian Intuition. C I Lewis Given. John Vervaeke "relevance realization. Pure Intuition is akin to Locke's primary qualities. The beheld is "recognized as". Sentient praxis uses beholding as one of its tools. Qualia has reverted to a version of sense-data in analytic philosophy. To say there is no beholding is to say there is no sentience. There are no vertebrates with complex nervous systems. This is an esoteric philosophical position which has, unfortunately become popular among a certain type of materialist-idealist sophisticates. And so the border between the esoteric and the exoteric is blurred.
