Definition

techne (τέχνη) — craft, skill, art

  • is substantive
  • a _thing you have or practice_
  • socially transmissible (teachable craft)

τεχνικός (_technikos_) — the adjective

  • is qualitative
  • _how_ something is done
  • implies:
  • precision
  • method
  • expertise
  • a doctor practices a _techne_
  • a doctor may act _technikos_ (skillfully, professionally)
