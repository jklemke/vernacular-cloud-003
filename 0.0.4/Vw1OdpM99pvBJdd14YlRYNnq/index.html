Definition

The jurisdiction of natural science. "Materialism" rightly understood. Methodological naturalism as applied science.

Natural language is not an analytic syntax; it is a suspensional grammar. One of the practices of natural scientific language is to create tentative, conjectura, calculational syntaxes for particular jurisdictions. This must be supported by an "out of band" programme to legitimate or deligitimate the jurisdiction.

Science is an articulated, social version of sentient praxis.
