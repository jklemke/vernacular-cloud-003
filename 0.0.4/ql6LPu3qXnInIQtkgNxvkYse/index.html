Definition

Solving a logical conundrum by positing a universal dictator.

  • First stage: explicate the conundrum of Infinity
  • Second stage: Leap to the Lord

To move from a logical conundrum to an other-worldly aristocrat. It is to say you are proving God, when what you're doing is legitimizing the LORD ALMIGHTY.

The proper response: that's an interesting logical puzzle, but it's got nothing to do with aristocracy. A logical puzzle is not the same thing as a shepherd Lord.

Because there are preconditions, there must be a God.
