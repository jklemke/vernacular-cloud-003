Definition

The arrow of time; actualization of outcome from multiple (uncountable infinite) preconditions and constrained possible outcomes.

!Victor Badinage Quotes by Theme#^46fdb3

The important distinction is between "cause" at the token level versus type level. We define "cause" to mean abstract, generalized patterns, and "contingent" to mean concrete particular actualizations.

The puzzlement over causality is a puzzle over the antinomy of Absolute Necessity
