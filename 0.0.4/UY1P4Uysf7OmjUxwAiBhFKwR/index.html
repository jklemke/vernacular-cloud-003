Definition

If any concept is ineffable, undefinable, preconditional, a priori, foundational, or presuppositional then time certainly is.

For Kant, space and time are the structural preconditions (_Formen_) through which perceptions are organized; space and time themselves cannot be perceived.

For Kant time and space are pure preconditions of sentient practice.

Time has been powerless to put its kindly philosophy out of fashion.
