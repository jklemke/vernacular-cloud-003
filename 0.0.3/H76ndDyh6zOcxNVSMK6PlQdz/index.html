concept: Myth of the Haven

Definition

Is it possible to teach to the young the subtle difference between mysticism and hypocrisy? Between gnosticism and dogmatism? Can we teach the young to oubliate the exoteric meaning of their culture, in order to luxuriate in the esoteric? Can we teach our emancipation as piety to the next generation? Can modernism be taught as a kind of piety? Can creative destruction be taught as the Myth of the Haven? If we invest in it the public good, will it allow the young to thrive in the sublime?
