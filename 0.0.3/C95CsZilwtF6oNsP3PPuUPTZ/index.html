concept: ostension

concept-key: https://vernacular.cloud/0.0.3/C95CsZilwtF6oNsP3PPuUPTZ/

Definition

The practice of verbalizing the foundational practices of IPSEITY and [SOCIETY]

The downtown of language is ostension: by which we mean the grammaticalization of sentient practice **and** social practice. But ostensive language is not fact-stating; it is possibility stating. It is for indexing a position in a corroborative scenario. The stipulate is implicit in the pragmatics of the formulation. And natural language has words that allow the stipulate to be made explicit--says, believes, knows.

The downtown of language is _situating_ aspects of objects in a scenario. Modal description. Alethic modality.

Metalanguage, metaphysics, suspensional language attempts to articulate the preconditions of ostensive language. Transcendent language attempts to articulate the intersubjective concepts as if they were ostensive.
