Definition

That which is not. the vacuum, the negation, the unnoticed, the oubliated, the irrelevant.

The Socratic notion of wisdom, to know that I do not know.

Nagarjuna, all is empty.

The word "not" shows us the centrality of the apophatic in everyday speech. Parmenides: "nothing exists", "nothing does not exist". Articulation as description versus articulation as apophasis. Parmenides, sophist. Accept "justice", "negation", "reference" as abstract terms, is that the same as god".

The paramaterized interpretive template starts with the void object. This is the Kantian empty concept. The isolate as a parameterized object.

Do unobservables exist? Can the void be real? How do we distinguish unobservable reality from the void. Or does existence mean not void?

Is it possible to conceive of a world without minds? Without subjectivity?
