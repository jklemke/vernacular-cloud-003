concept: enaction

concept-key: https://vernacular.cloud/0.0.3/Uj5ezPtqhmRdlb9AL0DqO6IQ/

Definition

The process by which an organism **brings forth (enacts) a meaningful world through its embodied, goal-directed interactions with its environment**. Enaction is recursive activity in the service of a norm, the recursive satisfaction of a norm.

Enaction is the view that cognition arises through **dynamic, reciprocal coupling between organism and environment, such that perception, action, and meaning are co-constituted in ongoing activity.
