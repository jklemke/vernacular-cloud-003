Definition

The provider is the Name of God as the source of what sustains: daily bread, rain in its season, the yield of the land.

Is there a provider? No, but we are provided for: by metabolism, by ecology, and by one another. The norms of survival and flourishing, and the bonds of commerce and sociality, do the providing that the Name gathers.

History Note

  • Abraham names the place "The LORD will provide" (Genesis 22:14).
  • The Stoics' pronoia, providence ordering the world for the good of rational beings (Cicero, On the Nature of the Gods II). SEP: Stoicism
  • Leibniz: this is the best of all possible worlds (Theodicy, 1710).
  • Adam Smith's invisible hand, a providence without a provider (The Wealth of Nations IV.2, 1776).

Scope Note

Providence here is provision, not control (see controller). The provider is the most practical of the Names: its work is done every day by metabolism, ecology, commerce, and care.
